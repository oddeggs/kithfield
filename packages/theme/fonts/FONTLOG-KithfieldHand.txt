Kithfield Hand — FONTLOG

Kithfield Hand is a modified version of Gaegu Bold (Copyright 2018 The Gaegu
Project Authors, JIKJI SOFT), released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(see OFL.txt). Renamed as the licence asks for modified versions.

1.002 (2026-10-19)
- Made the marks consistent: every mark is now solved to a shared stroke
  weight (lowercase and capitals each have one) and matching widths, so
  the breve, circumflex and tilde are the same size and weight, and tones
  no longer vary between heavy and hairline.
- Grave now sits to the right of the circumflex, like acute and hook.
- Hook above is heavier, with its curl kept open.

1.001 (2026-10-19)
- Redrew the horn (ơ ư Ơ Ư): a thickened, curved stroke growing from the
  shoulder, replacing the rotated comma. ơ/ư are now bases with their own
  tone position, so tone marks clear the horn.
- Tilde is now the same stroke weight as the letters.

1.000 (2026-10-19) — OddEggs (Vit Lon Studio)
- Added all 134 Vietnamese letters (upper and lower case) as composites.
- Added combining marks U+0300 U+0301 U+0302 U+0303 U+0306 U+0309 U+031B
  U+0323, with capital (.case) variants and stacked circumflex/breve + tone
  combinations.
- Marks were derived from Gaegu's own glyphs so they share its hand:
  acute/grave from the apostrophe, circumflex from ^, tilde from ~, hook above
  from the hook of ?, dot below from the period, breve from (, horn from the
  comma, the Đ/đ bar from the hyphen. Middle dot (U+00B7) from the period.
- Added dotless i (U+0131).
- OpenType: ccmp (dotless i, Vietnamese mark stacking, capital marks),
  mark and mkmk positioning so decomposed (NFD) text renders the same as
  precomposed. Existing fwid feature kept unchanged.
- Vertical metrics unchanged from Gaegu Bold; every Vietnamese letter fits
  inside the line box (tallest: Ẳ at 751 of 760).
- All original Gaegu glyphs, including Hangul, are unchanged.
- New glyphs are unhinted.
