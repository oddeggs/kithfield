Motion
16-0116-02
Any member can light the fire. Lighting it sets a farm-wide flag: the farm scene shows a flame by the fire ring and the speaker's critter, and the widget reads "Pip and Bea are by the fire". No push, no ring, no badge. The fire goes out 3 minutes after the last person leaves.
Glow fades in over 1.2s. Flame redraws on twos. Embers flicker at 1.3 rad/s when unlit.
16-0316-0416-0516-06
Join in or Just listen; both arrive muted. Listening never opens the mic and shows an ear chip. Gestures (wave, dewdrop, hug, stickers) float over the receiver's critter for 3s and count as tending. Mouth and ears react to that person's level; laughter lifts the flame by up to 30%. Max 24 voices, 8 seats drawn; more sit as a ring of small lanterns.
Your critter walks the path to a free log in ~2s, on twos, then sits. Speaking tilts the critter 2° toward the fire. Gestures pop in 2 drawings.
16-07
Weak connection (packet loss > 15% for 3s): that person's critter dozes, a moon chip shows "paused", their audio is paused rather than garbled. Rejoins automatically.
Doze uses the sleep drawing and zzz; waking is a single held frame swap.
16-0816-09
Leaving never announces. Your critter waves and walks home along the path. When the last person leaves, the fire settles to embers and the Live Activity ends.
Wave: 3 swings, then a 3s walk on twos. Quiet: flame shrinks over 4s; fireflies drift in.
16-1016-11
Plus: live captions and translation, on device. Free with the system Live Captions setting.
See motion row for captions.
16-1216-1316-14
Lantern (camera) is off by default and asks once (10-13). Layouts: 2 people big frames, 3–4 a 2×2 grid, 6–8 frames sit at each seat around the fire. Camera off shows the critter in the same frame, animated (lip-sync and expressions), with no "camera off" shaming copy beyond the name chip. Frames are round with a hand-drawn ring; a lit frame has a tiny lantern on the rim.
Frame swaps between video and critter cross-fade 200ms. Talking adds a honey name chip, never a glowing ring.
16-1516-16
Screen share is one at a time and shows in a big hand-drawn card; video frames shrink to a row. Flipped camera ("look at this") moves the person's face to a small frame and labels the big one.
Card rises 320ms spring.
16-1716-1816-19
LATER. Critter mask: ARKit face tracking maps blend shapes to the five expressions and mouth openness; the camera image never leaves the phone. Visit calls: a knock invite with Come sit, Not now, or a sticker reply. It expires after 60s into a single soft line; no ringtone, no missed-call count.
Knock: door wobbles twice. Expressions hold at least 1.5s.
