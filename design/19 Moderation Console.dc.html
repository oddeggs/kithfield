Motion
19-01
Internal web app at safety.kithfield.com behind SSO plus hardware keys; roles: moderator, senior (bans, legal escalation), legal. Queue order: urgent (self-harm, child safety, credible threats) first with a 1-hour target, then oldest first with a 24-hour target. Auto-flags (link reputation, hash matching) enter the same queue; nothing is removed by automation alone except known CSAM hashes. Rows are claimed on open to prevent double handling. Empty state: "All clear. Go and get some air."
Static. Row hover tint 120ms. No sounds, no confetti.
19-02
Opening a report decrypts the reported item and up to 10 messages either side using a per-report escrow key (report payload is encrypted to the safety key on the reporter’s device), and logs the access. Images and video stay blurred until "Show". Actions: dismiss, remove, remove + warn, pause 7 days, remove from a farm, remove from Kithfield (second moderator must approve), escalate (urgent or legal). Every action needs a rule. Apply sends 11-21 to reporters and 11-22 to the member as in-farm system notes, never push text with the content.
Selected action highlights 150ms. Apply button colour changes with severity (ink, then brick for removal).
19-03
Shows only safety history and reported items. Warnings decay after 6 months clean. Pauses make the critter nap and disable posting; removal from Kithfield turns every cottage to a sketch (04-23) and blocks re-signup by account, Apple/Google ID and device check.
Timeline rows fade in 30ms apart on first load.
19-04
Appeals are assigned only to moderators who did not touch the original report (enforced server-side). Outcomes: keep, or undo and restore the message with an apology note. 7-day target. Overturns feed back into rule guidance.
None.
19-05
Append-only log (WORM storage), retained 2 years; reports and evidence kept 1 year unless under legal hold. Child sexual abuse material: hash-match on upload (PhotoDNA / NCMEC hash list), block, preserve and report to NCMEC as US law requires; never shown to moderators unblurred. Moderator wellbeing: graphic exposure timer, enforced breaks, opt-out lanes. Public contact: safety@kithfield.com, in-app 11-17 and 11-21, and on 17-12.
Static.
