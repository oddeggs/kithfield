Farm draws in (12-01)
UI chrome (sheets, buttons, lists) moves on smooth curves. Art never tweens: it changes by swapping whole drawings on twos, which is what makes it feel hand-made. With Reduce Motion, art holds still and UI uses motion.fade only.
Art rules
Drawn in code with the hand-drawn canvas techniques: ink, pencil, wash and gouache on paper.
Ink
Brush-pen lines are filled ribbons whose width follows pen pressure, tapering at both ends. Closed shapes start at a random point and overlap their start.
Pencil
Fur, wood grain, wool and texture use narrow, broken graphite passes. Never on outlines of critters.
Wash
Watercolour sits 1–2px off the line, multiplies, granulates and darkens at its drying edge.
Gouache
Critter bodies are opaque so the paper never shows through a face.
Paper
Every scene starts on warm paper with tooth and fibres. Nothing is ever flat colour.
Night
Night is a dark pass multiplied over the day drawing, with light cut out of it, never a separate palette of art.
Whole drawings
Critters are cached whole drawings (open, blink, sleep), held on twos. No rigs, no puppet tweening.
One gesture
Stickers and spots carry one gesture and one prop, readable at 40px.

Notes for the developer
Shared motion tokens live in 01 Foundations.
Screen
States & interactions
Motion
Tokens
Ship colours as an asset catalog with Any / Dark appearances using the day and night columns. Type ramps map to UIFont.TextStyle so Dynamic Type works everywhere.
motion.* map to SwiftUI: sheet = .spring(response: 0.32, dampingFraction: 0.9), fade = .easeOut(duration: 0.2), cross = .linear(duration: 0.12).
Art
kithfield-art.js is the reference renderer. Production can port it to Core Graphics or pre-render sprites per critter, outfit and size; scenes cache their background and redraw only the moving layer.
Art ticks at 12fps (on twos), paused off screen and fully still with Reduce Motion.
Components
Buttons, chips, fields, bubbles, sheets, tab bar, tray and cards are the complete set; screens never introduce one-off styles.
Press state: scale 0.97 and 8% darker, 90ms.
