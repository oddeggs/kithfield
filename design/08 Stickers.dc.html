Everyone gets a free animated pack of their own critter, and it always wears what their critter has on. Together stickers put friends' real critters side by side. Notes for the developer
Shared motion tokens live in 01 Foundations.
Screen
States & interactions
Motion
08-01
Tabs: Mine, Together, Farm, packs. Mine always matches what your critter is wearing right now, so an outfit change re-renders the pack. Tap to send; long-press previews at full size. Recent row holds 8.
Sheet rises 320ms spring (0.9 damping). Stickers loop at 12fps; the picker pauses any that are off screen.
08-02
Pick up to 4 friends from this farm. Blocked or removed members never appear. Each friend gets a small "you're in a sticker" note the first time, and can opt out in 11-02.
Faces hop into the frame one after another, 80ms apart.
08-03
Stickers sit beside the text chips in Quick Tend. Sending one marks the card done and fades it to 72%.
Sent sticker shrinks into the card (200ms) and the card dims.
08-04
Packs are owned forever. "With Plus" packs show a small badge but can still be bought on their own. Prices load from the App Store; show a quiet "Prices unavailable" line when offline.
Pack rows preview two stickers that loop only while on screen.
08-0508-06
A gifted pack arrives as a parcel on the doorstep. The receiver opens it; the sender sees "Bea opened it" in the chat, never a read time.
Parcel string unties (2 drawings), lid lifts, stickers tumble out on twos.
