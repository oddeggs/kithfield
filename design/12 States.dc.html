Tone marks: switch the whole stack
Young Serif and Gaegu ship without Vietnamese. Letting the browser fall back glyph by glyph mixes two typefaces inside one word. For vi, swap the whole stack: Lora for display, Patrick Hand for handwriting. Body text stays SF Pro, which covers Vietnamese.

Motion
12-01
Show the farm reveal for loads over 300ms; under that, no loader at all. Sheet rows are pencil-dashed outlines, not grey shimmer.
Reveal: wobbly blob grows from the farm's centre over 4.2s with a pencil edge. With Reduce Motion, the finished farm cross-fades in over 200ms.
12-02 / 12-03
Empty farm shows sketched cottages and an Invite button. Empty search suggests searching all farms.
Spot critter breathes; the prop bobs.
12-04
Offline: fog over the farm, banner explains, outgoing items queue in the post bag and send in order on reconnect. Reading cached messages still works.
Mist drifts 6–12px/s. Fog lifts over 1.5s when back online.
12-05
Full-screen error only when nothing is cached. Otherwise, a one-line banner over the last good farm. Codes are shown small for support.
Rain cloud drips on loop.
12-06
Uploads continue in a background URLSession. Each item has its own ring and can be cancelled. "Send smaller copies" re-encodes at 1600px.
Rings fill continuously (the only continuous motion in the app, so progress feels honest).
12-07
Hints: tap a critter, long-press a message, plant a seed. Stored per device.
Spotlight eases in over 240ms; tip card rises 8px.
12-08 / 12-09
Night palette: sky #1b1f3d, panels #23263f, bubbles #2f3352, text #f4ecda. Lit windows only for friends around.
Fireflies drift; stars twinkle at 0.8 rad/s.
12-10
Reduce Motion: every kf-art gets the still attribute; sheets and pushes cross-fade 200ms; no parallax; sticker loops show their first frame and play once on tap.
None by design.
12-11
At AX1–AX5 the farm header shrinks to 150pt, the title shortens to the farm's short name, tab labels hide (icons stay with large-content viewer), and bubbles go full width. All text uses Dynamic Type styles.
Same as standard.
12-12
Locale vi: swap display to Lora 600 and hand to Patrick Hand. Allow 2-line labels. Dates use Vietnamese formats via the system formatter.
Same as standard.
