Notes for the developer
Shared motion tokens live in 01 Foundations.
Screen
States & interactions
Motion
04-01
Farm canvas on top (540pt), sheet below with credits and plans. Tap a critter for 04-06, a cottage for its owner, the fire ring for 06-07. Day/night follows local sunset; the night tweak previews it.
Clouds drift 2–3px/s, chimney smoke loops 4.5s, critters breathe and blink. Season changes cross-fade the whole scene over 1.2s.
04-02
One list, sorted by last activity. Farms show a thumbnail of their land; visits show both critters. Swipe left: mute, pin to top. A dozing farm reads in plain text, never "inactive".
New items slide in from the top (220ms). Honey dot fades in, never pulses.
04-03 / 04-04
Header farm collapses from 236pt to 150pt as you scroll. Dewdrop chip toggles on tap. Swipe right on any bubble to reply. Max 24 members per farm.
Header height tracks scroll 1:1, then the critters settle in 2 drawings. New messages rise 12px and fade in over 180ms.
04-0504-07
Card sheet: Visit, Firefly, Knock, Gift, Photos, More (mute, report, block). A visit is end-to-end like the farm chat and invisible to other members.
Card sheet 320ms spring. The porch lantern sways ±3° on a 4s loop.
04-08
Reply chip above the field, with ✕ to cancel. Typing @ opens the member list filtered as you type; dozing members are listed last. A mention nudges only that person, once.
Mention list grows from the caret (160ms).
04-09
Own message menu: Reply, Edit (15 minutes), Pin, Plant as a seed, Copy, Unsend (any time). Others' messages: Reply, Pin, Plant, Copy, Report.
Background blurs to 12px over 200ms, bubble lifts with a 1.04 scale.
04-10
Up to 10 pins; the oldest drops off with a note. Tap a card to jump to the message.
Cards drop onto the cork with a 2-drawing settle and a ±2° tilt.
04-11
Search runs on device across messages, photos, links and voice transcripts (when on). Filters by person and farm. Empty result uses 12-03.
Results cross-fade per keystroke (120ms), no layout jumps.
04-12
Hold the mic to record; slide left to cancel, slide up to lock. Max 5 minutes. Waveform is live. Releasing sends.
Your critter sings in the corner: mouth opens on the input level, on twos.
04-13
Swipe between items, pinch to zoom, swipe down to close. Video has a scrubber and plays muted until tapped.
Opens with a zoom from the bubble (280ms spring); closes back into it.
04-14
Grouped by month. Long-press a tile to save or forward.
Grid tiles fade in as they decode, 120ms.
04-15
States: Sending (60% opacity, clock), Sent (single tick), Failed (clay border, Try again). Edited and unsent labels. Link previews are fetched on the sender's phone. Typing shows the critter writing a letter for each person typing, max 2, then "and others".
Letter scribbles grow line by line (2.5s loop). Failed bubble shakes once, 3px.
04-16 / 04-17
Welcome back after 7+ days away. Valley switcher shows every farm you're on; tap to switch, + to plant a new one.
Valley pans with the finger; farms bob gently.
04-18
Name 1–32 characters. Land picks the base palette. Creator becomes keeper.
Selected land tile scales 1.04.
04-19
Shown once to everyone on the farm the next time they open it, then becomes a chat line.
Bunting sways, confetti falls on twos for 4s, critters hop in turn.
04-2004-22
Leaving sends no notification. The cottage dozes for 30 days, then a keeper may sketch it back. Removal (04-21) is shown once in app; we never say who did it.
Lamp goes out with a 400ms fade; the lantern on the step flickers.
04-23
Statuses last 24 hours, 40 characters.
Bubbles bob 2px on a 3s loop.
