06-01
Day, place and enough yeses (a couple, most, everyone). Seed grows a stage per condition. Place autocompletes from Apple Maps; location permission is never needed to type one.
Seed → sprout → bud → bloom swaps on held frames with a 2-drawing wiggle.
06-02
Each day option is a bud; votes make it grow. Tap to vote, tap again to unvote. The sun patch marks the day most people can do.
Buds grow 14pt per vote with a 240ms spring.
06-03
Whoever wears the hat gets the gentle nudges for missing RSVPs, so nobody chases friends. The hat can be passed.
Hat hops from critter to critter on pass (2 drawings).
06-04
Sprouted plans first, then growing, then needs-something, then resting seeds ("someday"). Add to calendar uses EventKit with only the event written.
Bloom glints twinkle.
06-05
A seed with a reminder ("interview Thursday") nudges friends the day after to ask how it went. Only visible to the farm.
Envelope wobble on arrival.
06-06
On the day: I'm here, Add a photo, Directions. Critters walk in as people arrive. After, the photos press into the scrapbook (07-06).
Fire flickers on twos, light radius flickers at 5 Hz. Arriving critters walk in along the path, hop on twos.
Live Activity with compact, minimal and expanded presentations. Expanded offers Mute and Leave. Ends when you leave or after 10 minutes alone.
