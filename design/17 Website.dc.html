17-08Privacy
kithfield.com/privacy
KithfieldEN · VI
Our privacy promise
Last updated 1 October 2026. The short version: your messages are yours, we can't read them, and we never sell or advertise with your data.
What we keep
Your account (email or phone), your critter and farms you belong to, and encrypted message envelopes until they're delivered. Photos and voice notes are encrypted end to end.
What we never do
No ads, no data brokers, no reading your chats, no recording by the fire. Captions and translation run on your device.
Your choices
Download your keepsakes or delete your account any time, in the app or on the web.
17-09Terms (house rules)
kithfield.com/terms
KithfieldEN · VI
House rules
Kithfield is for friend groups who already know each other. You must be 13 or older (16 where local law requires).
Be kind on every farm
No bullying, hate, harassment or unwanted contact. Keepers can remove critters; we can close accounts that break these rules.
Purchases
Plus and items are sold through the App Store and Google Play. Items you buy are yours for as long as Kithfield runs.
Leaving
You can leave any farm quietly, or delete your account. We delete your data within 30 days.

17-01 – 17-04
Static site (Astro or plain HTML) with one shared kithfield-art.js module, the same file the app uses for its reference renders. Breakpoints: mobile under 600, tablet 600–1099, desktop 1100+. Language from the URL (/vi), with the switcher in the nav and hreflang tags. Vietnamese swaps Young Serif → Lora and Gaegu → Patrick Hand for the whole page.
Every kf-art is lazy: it mounts on IntersectionObserver (200px margin), draws its background once and ticks at 12fps only while visible. Hero farm loads first. prefers-reduced-motion adds the still attribute everywhere. Budget: under 120 KB of JS before art, LCP is the hero headline (text, not canvas).
Cast
Hover (desktop) or tap (touch) a tile: that critter switches to the wave pose and its one-line personality appears below. Keyboard: tiles are buttons; focus behaves like hover.
Pose swap is a held-frame change, no tween.
17-05
Invite page (kithfield.com/f/slug) is server-rendered with the farm name and inviter for the OG card. "Come in from the browser" opens the working web farm (14-09); app buttons deep-link with the invite token. Unknown or expired links show a friendly "This gate is closed" page.
Saved cottage pulses its glow.
