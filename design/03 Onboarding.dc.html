Notes for the developer
Shared motion tokens live in 01 Foundations.
Screen
States & interactions
Motion
03-01
Two paths: Start a farm, or I have an invite (opens a paste field that also reads the clipboard once, with the system paste prompt). Returning users sign in from the same button.
Gate swings open 2 drawings after load. Clouds drift 3px/s. Critters breathe.
03-02
Sign in with Apple uses the system button style and the Hide My Email option. Google uses the standard OAuth sheet. Email sends a 6-digit code. Errors appear as one plain line under the buttons: "That didn't work. Try again or use email."
Buttons fade up 60ms apart (200ms ease-out).
03-03
One-time code autofill from Mail (textContentType oneTimeCode). Auto-submits on the 6th digit. Wrong code: boxes shake once (3px, 2 cycles) and clear. Codes last 10 minutes; resend is always available, with no countdown shown.
Owl bobs; the envelope floats on a 1.6 rad/s sine.
03-0403-05
Filter by shape. Picking a critter resets the coat. The twin check compares against existing farm members.
Selection ring snaps (no tween). Stage critter swaps on a held frame, then waves once.
03-0603-0803-1103-12
Our explainer comes first, then the iOS alert. "Not now" never asks again in this session; we ask once more only when the feature is used. Each screen has a Skip that leaves the feature off with no nag.
Spot critter breathes; prop floats with a soft glow.
03-07
Farm name is required (1–32 characters). The invite link is created immediately; Share opens the system share sheet. A farm holds up to 24 critters (see 11-07).
Sketched cottages pulse their "?" at 1.4 rad/s until a friend joins.
03-09
Contact matching uses hashed numbers on device. Invite sends a link by Messages. Already-invited rows show Invited. Done jumps to the move-in (04-19) when anyone is already here, otherwise to the farm.
Invite chip flips to Invited with a 150ms cross-fade.
03-10
Universal link opens the App Store with the farm name passed through; after install, the app lands straight on 03-02 with the farm pre-selected.
None beyond the critters.
03-13
E.164 number with country picker defaulting to the device region. SMS code reuses 03-03 (autofill from Messages). Discoverability by number is opt-in and can be changed in 11-02.
Same as 03-03.
03-14
Age gate after sign-in, before the critter picker: 13+, or the local digital-consent age (e.g. 16 in parts of the EU). Under-age: a kind "Come back when you're older" screen and the account is not created. Year is never shown to anyone.
Rose sways ±2°.
