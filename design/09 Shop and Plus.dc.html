Shop & patrons
Kithfield is open source with no ads, and the shop pays for the servers. So what's on Rocco's cart has to be worth wanting: cottages you'd move into, companions, rare coats, and little things that react to your friends.
Care is always free
Chat, tending, gestures, plans, the fire and your own sticker pack never sit behind a paywall.
Worth wanting
Hand-painted, seasonal, and back round again. You buy what you'd gift a friend.
Friends miss nothing
A friend who never buys a thing sees everything you wear and visits every cottage.
Open books
Open source, no ads, no data selling. We publish what the farm costs every month.
Never: loot boxes or random paid draws, countdowns or "only today", sale prices or strike-throughs, water, growth or wake-ups for sale, or anything that makes a friend without money look worse off.

Lamps instead of online dots: "lamp's on" for heads-down time

09-01
Weekly rotation of 8–12 items. Every item returns at least twice a year; tiles say when it was last here or comes back. No timers anywhere.
Cart rolls in once per visit (900ms, on twos).
09-02, 09-20
Live try-on renders the item on your real farm with a Day/Night toggle; the widget preview renders tonight's widget with it. Nothing is shared until kept.
Held-frame swap; windmill sails turn 0.6 rad/s; lighthouse beam sweeps at night.
09-13 – 09-16
Cottage styles replace the cottage drawing everywhere (farm, night, widget, valley). Companions attach to anchors (follow, shoulder, lantern). Rare coats are seasonal palettes for any critter. Reactive items subscribe to farm events: dewdrop → chime, chat activity → scarf, fire room → teacup, local rain → umbrella.
Chime rings 900ms; scarf flutters while messages arrived in the last 30s; steam loops; umbrella opens in 2 drawings.
09-17, 09-21
Sets are fixed lists; completing one unlocks a sticker and a pose. The collection lists everything owned and where it came from (bought, gift, patron).
Set reward pops once with a sparkle.
09-06, 09-18, 09-19
Wishes show on your critter card to farm members only. Group gifts collect private pledges, charge only when covered, and refund automatically if not covered by the end of the birthday.
Progress fills continuously.
09-04, 09-05
Five free wrapping styles. The gift waits on the step until opened.
See 18-09.
09-07
Farm themes are shared: anyone on the farm can chip in, the theme switches on when covered and stays forever for that farm.
Theme change cross-fades the farm over 1.2s.
09-03, 09-22
Patron (monthly or yearly), Farm Plus (one payer, up to 8 members get perks), Founding Farmer (lifetime, sold only in the launch season, then retired; no countdown shown). StoreKit 2 and Play Billing.
