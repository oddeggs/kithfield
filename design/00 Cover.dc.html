Kithfield
a cosy group chat where friends are critters on a shared farm
An iPhone app for any friend group. Each friend is a critter living on the farm, and talking to each other tends it. Kith is an old word for friends and neighbours; Kithfield is the field you keep together.
243 screens18 files16 critters
The rules
Every screen keeps these. If a design breaks one, it is wrong.
01
Everything counts as tending
Replies, reactions, photos and voice notes, and one-tap gestures that need no reply: a dewdrop, a firefly, a note marked no reply needed.
02
Sleepy, never dead
Quiet friends' critters doze. Coming back is always met with a warm welcome, never guilt.
03
No streaks, counters or read receipts
New activity is a honey dot. "Sent" means delivered, nothing more.
04
Plans live outside the chat
"We should hang out" becomes a seed that sprouts with a date, a place and enough yeses.
05
Quiet care shows
The farm credits the friends who keep the group going, including the ones who mostly listen.
06
Calm by default
At most one push a day per farm, and they back off when ignored. Care is never behind a paywall.
