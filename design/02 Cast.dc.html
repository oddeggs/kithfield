Notes for the developer
Shared motion tokens live in 01 Foundations.

States & interactions
Motion
Sheets
Critters render from kithfield-art.js as cached sprites keyed by kind, coat, marking, outfit, state and size. Open, blink and sleep are separate drawings. Outfits sit on named anchors (top, neck, eyes, ear) so new accessories never need per-critter art.
Breathing is a 3.1s sine on scale (1.5%); sleeping slows to 4.4s and 2.8%. Blinks last 130ms every 3.4–5.5s, staggered per critter. Never ease a critter across the screen: they hop on twos.
Poses
Awake, sleepy, wave, hug, at the fire, walking and widget size. The picker (03-04) and every farm use the same drawings.
Wave: paw swings ±0.5 rad at 9 rad/s. Hug: 5 rad/s squeeze, 8%. Walking: 5 rad/s hop with dust puffs. Fire: flame redraws on twos with a 5 Hz flicker on the light radius.
Twins
When a new critter matches someone already on the farm (same kind, coat, marking and accessory), the picker suggests a change. It never blocks.
The two faces slide together and overlap 16px.
