Notes for the developer
Shared motion tokens live in 01 Foundations.
Screen
States & interactions
Motion
05-01
Reactions: dewdrop, sun, sprout, wave, firefly. One per person; tapping again removes it. Menu adds Reply, Plant as a seed, Keep in the scrapbook, Copy, Report.
Tray scales from the bubble (0.9 → 1, 200ms spring). Chosen reaction pops 1.12 and drops onto the bubble in 2 drawings.
05-02
Gesture cards: Dewdrop, Firefly, Wave, Quiet note (no reply needed), Plant a seed. Photo, Voice and Doodle below. First photo asks permission (03-11).
Sheet 320ms spring; cards stagger in 40ms apart.
05-03
Pick a door; the firefly waits on that step until they open the app, then floats to their window. Optional few words.
Firefly glow pulses at 2.4 rad/s. On send it drifts along a curve to the door over 1.2s, on twos.
05-04
Three waiting cards (questions, plans, someone back), the rest folded into a bouquet. Tapping a chip or sticker sends and dims the card; tap again to undo for 5s.
Sent card fades to 72%. Bouquet ribbon wiggles when it gains a stem.
05-05
Slow post delivers at the friend's next morning (their time zone). Can be recalled until it leaves.
Your critter walks the path with a lantern overnight, on twos; the letter lands with a flap.
05-06
Knock wobbles the friend's widget window once; no sound, no banner. Limited to one knock per friend per day.
Window wobble: 3 swings, ±4°, 600ms.
05-07
Raise a rain cloud over your cottage to say "rough day". Friends can bring an umbrella (a gesture) or a visit. Clears after 48h or when you lower it.
Rain loops at 12fps; umbrellas pop open in 2 drawings.
05-08
Share extension: photos, video, links, text and files. Recent farms and visits first; multi-select allowed. Sends in the background. Photos count as sunflower dew on each farm.
Extension sheet uses the system share animation.
05-09
Forward to any farms and visits (up to 5). The original sender gets one quiet note naming where it went (never for visits). Messages from blocked people can't be forwarded.
Selected rows tick in 120ms.
05-10
Files via the document picker, up to 100 MB, encrypted. Upload progress in the bubble, Quick Look on tap, save to Files on long-press.
Progress bar fills continuously.
05-11
Twelve reactions, each with a plain meaning shown under it the first few times. One per person per message; the long-press tray (05-01) shows the five most used.
Picked reaction pops 1.12 and drops onto the bubble in 2 drawings.
