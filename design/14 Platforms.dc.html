Notes for the developer
Shared motion tokens live in 01 Foundations.
Screen
States & interactions
Motion
14-01, 14-02
NavigationSplitView with three columns in landscape (farms and visits | chat | live farm); portrait collapses the farm into the chat header as on iPhone. Large-screen farm (14-02) is the farm full screen with the chat as a floating trailing panel; Stage Manager windows reflow between the two at 900pt.
Same as iPhone. The live farm reacts to new messages: the sender's critter holds up a speech bubble for 4s and does its message pose (blanket, cake, wave) on twos.
14-03, 14-04
iPhone Duo: compact width on the outer display (farm | chat with controls on the trailing side, per the vertical-controls pattern), regular width inside (farm | chat as a split arrangement). Keep the same state across the hinge. When partially folded, panes go equal width and the folding region (ReservedRegion) stays clear of bubbles and the composer.
No extra motion when folding: only the pane widths change, over the system transition.
14-05 – 14-08
Android: Material 3 navigation bar, FAB for the dewdrop, top app bar with a back arrow, predictive back gesture to the farm. Notifications use MessagingStyle with direct actions, in two channels. Widgets via Glance in 4×2 and 2×2. Fonts: Roboto replaces SF Pro; Young Serif and Gaegu stay. Art renders from the same engine.
Predictive back previews the farm behind the chat with a 120px shade. Widget updates are static frames.
14-09, 14-10
Invite links open a working farm on the web without an account: pick a critter, name it, chat right away as a guest. Signing in later keeps the critter. Under 600px the web uses the phone layout (14-10) with a bottom bar; over 1100px it uses the three panes.
The saved cottage glows on a 1.6s pulse until you move in.
14-11 – 14-14
Desktop (Mac, Windows, web at ≥1100px): three resizable panes. Menu bar (Mac) or tray (Windows) critter glows when a firefly is waiting and opens a popover with the night widget. Drag photos anywhere onto the chat. Notifications are soft: banners only for doorstep items, plans and @mentions, never sound by default. Shortcuts: ⌘D dewdrop, ⌘⇧T Quick Tend, : for stickers, ⌘K search, ⌘/ list. Night mode follows the system appearance or local sunset.
Tray glow breathes over 2.4s while a firefly waits. Drop zone fades in at 120ms. Sticker suggestions update per keystroke (cross-fade 120ms).
14-15
Desktop fire room replaces the live farm in the right pane while you're in it; the chat stays usable. Menu-bar (or tray) critter glows warm when friends are by the fire, with a popover to come sit. ⌥⌘M mutes, ⌥⌘L joins or leaves. Push-to-talk with Space while the pane is focused.
Menu-bar glow breathes over 2.4s while a fire is lit. Pane swap cross-fades 200ms.
