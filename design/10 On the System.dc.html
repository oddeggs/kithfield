10-01
WidgetKit: small (fire or next plan), medium (farm at night), large (farm + who's up). Timeline refreshes on farm activity, max every 15 minutes; lit windows = opened in the last 3 hours. Tap opens the farm; tap a window opens that friend's card. Supports tinted and dark home screens.
Static per timeline entry. A knock plays the window wobble once via a pre-rendered entry.
10-02
Live Activity starts 2 hours before a sprouted plan and ends 2 hours after. Shows who's on their way and who's there.
Critter icons step along a path as people arrive (one step per update).
10-03 / 10-04
Push types: plan needs you, something on your doorstep, fire is lit today, birthday. Max one per farm per day, collapsed by farm. If 3 in a row go unopened, we pause that type for a week and say so in 11-08. Actions: I'm in, Can't this time, Leave a dewdrop.
Rich notification image is a still render of the seed or farm.
10-0510-07
Messages: sticker app extension reading the shared container, regenerated when the outfit changes. Telegram: animated WEBM set, 512px, created via the bot API on the user's confirmation. WhatsApp: animated WebP pack (3–30 stickers, 512px, 96px tray icon) through the add-to-WhatsApp intent. Together stickers need consent from every friend in them.
Exports render at 30fps from the same drawings, looping in 2s.
10-0810-09
Live Activity for the room: compact (flame + level bars), minimal (flame), expanded and lock screen (tiny critters around the flame, Mute and Leave). Starts when you sit down, ends when you leave or 10 minutes after you're alone. Never starts on someone else's phone.
The tiny fire flickers on twos; level bars follow the active speaker at 12fps.
10-10
AVPictureInPictureController with a custom round content view: the active speaker's lantern frame (or their critter if camera off), with your own critter in a small inset. Drag to corners; stash at the edge.
Swaps to the new active speaker with a 200ms cross-fade, held for at least 2s.
10-11
Route picker lists AirPods, iPhone, Speaker and AirPlay outputs (listen only over AirPlay). Removing an AirPod pauses your mic. Route changes show a 2s toast in the farm voice.
Toast drops 8pt and fades, 200ms.
10-1210-13
Mic is requested on first Join in, camera on first Light lantern. Our sheet first, then the iOS alert. Denied: Join in becomes Just listen with a quiet Settings link; never re-asks in the same session.
Spot critter breathes; prop floats.
10-14
Widget timeline gets a "fire lit" entry with the first two names. Reloads when the fire is lit or goes out. Tapping opens 16-03.
Static per entry.
