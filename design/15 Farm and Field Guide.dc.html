How the farm is calculated
Shared farm state
Each plant has a dew total per season. Stage = seed at 0, sprout at 3, bud at 8, bloom at 15, harvest at season end if it reached bloom. Dew comes from the table above, summed across members, with the per-person daily cap. The server is the source of truth; phones apply tends optimistically and reconcile.
Fairness
Each member's contribution is capped per day and per plant, so the farm reflects how many people cared, not how much one person typed. Credits (04-01) name kinds of care, never totals.
Weather
Local weather comes from the keeper's city (coarse, from the farm settings, never device location). Rain, frost and heat each give a 15% chance per day of a rare variant on blooming plants. Shared rain (07-03) gives a 40% chance.
Seasons
Meteorological seasons by hemisphere: north Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec, south Sep/Dec/Mar/Jun. Season change cross-fades the farm over 1.2s on next open. Harvest day is the last Sunday of each season.
Signature crop
Every farm gets one when planted, chosen from a pool (plums, quince, figs, damsons…) and named after the farm. It grows from all tending combined and can be traded on visits (15-06).
Field guide
A plant's page is "found" when the farm first reaches bloom. Rare variants get their own pressed page. Pages are shared by the farm; trades fill them on both farms.
