Farm rules
The logic behind the farm, for whoever builds it.
15-08Farm rules (for developers) · matches docs/farm-model.md, 18 Oct 2026
1
Principles
Derived, never set
The farm is computed from what friends did. One pure reducer in @kithfield/farm runs on the server, in the app and in widgets; the server result is the truth, phones apply their own tends at once and reconcile.
Time is computed
Sleepy and misty are worked out from timestamps and "now", never stored or scheduled. Randomness is a seeded hash of recorded facts, so every device agrees.
Only ever up
Stages only rise within a season. Nothing dies, wilts to brown or loses progress. Edits and unsends never take dew back: care given stays given.

Rose
Blooms over a member's cottage on their birthday, watered by anyone that day (07-01).
Signature crop
One per farm, grown from all care combined and named after the farm, like the Thursday plum. Tradeable on visits (15-06).
Rare variants
Rain-kissed, frost-lace and sun-gold are a property of a blooming plant with its own field guide page, not separate plants.
3
Growth
Cap
Each person adds at most 3 dew per plant per day (the farm's local day). Tends over the cap still count for sleepiness and credits; they add no dew.
Scaling
k = max(1, √(active ÷ 3)), where active is members who tended this season. Sprout at 3k, bud at 8k, bloom at 15k, rounded up. Stage = max(stored, from dew), so a new member never lowers a plant.
Harvest and the guide
On the last Sunday of the season (farm time) every plant that bloomed goes into the basket; the rest simply rest. A page is found the first time the farm brings a plant to bloom. Credits name kinds of care and people, never totals.

5
Seasons, weather and chance
Season
Meteorological, by the keeper's hemisphere: north Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec, south Sep/Dec/Mar/Jun. Every device computes it from the calendar until season_rolled lands.
Weather
Once a day the keeper city's weather (from farm settings, never device location) is written as a weather event: rain, frost, heat or none. Apple Weather attribution shows wherever it appears (15-09).
Shared rain and rare
3+ members opening within 10 minutes writes shared_rain (once a day) and grows a Gatherings tend for each. A bloom turns rare when hash(farmId, plantId, day, weather) < 0.15, or 0.40 with shared rain that day. It can't be rerolled.
6
Snapshots, versions and the porch pot
Snapshot
Plants, members with last-opened and last-tended times, season, weather, field guide and rules version, plus its seq. Widgets read it from the App Group and work out sleepy offline.
Rule changes
Every event records its rules_version. New rules apply from rules_changed on; past seasons are never recomputed.
Porch pot (visits)
One plant per season grown by both people: forget-me-not, nasturtium, cosmos, paperwhite. Same 3-dew cap, k = 1. Its blooms fill neither farm's guide; they press into the visit's own scrapbook (15-10, 15-11).

States & interactions
Motion
15-01
Opens on the current season. Tabs switch the page; rows are always the six families in the same order (Dew, Words, Pictures, Voices, Gestures, Gatherings). Status: found (bloomed once), growing (current season, with its stage), resting (season passed without bloom), not yet (grey pencil sketch). No counts. "All year" holds the rose, the signature crop and rare pages.
Tab change cross-fades rows 160ms; tiles hold still.
15-1015-11
One pot per visit, k = 1, same 3-dew cap per person per day. Any tend in the visit counts (no families). The pot follows the season: forget-me-not, nasturtium, cosmos, paperwhite. Blooms press into the visit scrapbook only. If either person blocks or leaves, the pot and scrapbook stay readable for the other, frozen.
The pot sways ±1° with the porch breeze; a new stage redraws over 3 held drawings.
15-09
WeatherKit attribution (required). Fetch WeatherService.shared.attribution and render its combinedMarkLightURL / combinedMarkDarkURL image (the mock shows it as text) plus a link to legalPageURL wherever weather changes the farm: the farm home weather chip and sheet, the widget's long-press detail, 09-16 umbrella detail and 11-17 About. Never alter or recolour the mark. Rain, frost and heat come from the keeper's city, fetched server-side once an hour and cached; the REST API attribution applies on web and Android too.
Rain falls on twos; umbrellas open over 400ms; the attribution row is static.
15-0115-02
Pages grouped by season tab. Unfound plants show the bud drawing as a grey pencil sketch with "not yet", never a lock or a count. Detail shows the six stages, who helped (names, no numbers), the sleepy rule and the rare variant.
Page turns: 2 drawings. The hero plant sways ±2° on a 4s loop; sleepy plants breathe slower.
15-03
Season is computed per farm from the keeper's hemisphere. The farm scene swaps palette, crops and roofs (snow in winter).
Season change cross-fades 1.2s on first open of the new season.
15-04
Shown once per rare bloom, the next time each member opens the farm. Variants: rain-kissed, frost-lace, sun-gold.
Rain loops at 12fps over the bloom; glints twinkle; the bloom opens in 3 held drawings.
15-0515-06
Harvest day is the last Sunday of the season. Trading needs both farms to have the crop at harvest; each trade is one bundle for one bundle, once per visit.
Basket fills one crop at a time, 150ms apart. Swap: bundles slide past each other, 400ms.
