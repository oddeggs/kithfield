rule

rule
Guests count toward "enough yeses". Their check-in on the day counts toward the hangout's Gatherings dew like anyone's.

Motion
Five new transitions, added to kf-motion.js alongside the seventeen in 18. Play, reverse, or tap the phone mid-flight to interrupt. With Reduce Motion each becomes a 200 ms cross-fade between its first and last frame.
M1A firefly sent from the widget
3.0 s. Tap ring 120 ms, the window glows once (spring), then the firefly drifts up and out. On device the widget plays a pre-rendered timeline entry; the send happens through an App Intent.
M2A lantern lighting, then dimming
4.2 s. Lights with a warm bloom in 400 ms; the dim at the end is slow (about 1 s) so a fading lantern never reads as an event.
M3The week page reveal
3.8 s. The harvest basket, then the page turns in from the left edge, then photos drop on one by one (70 ms stagger, spring).
M4The project's overnight reveal
4.2 s. Evening, night falls over the scaffolding, morning shows the finished build with a single sparkle. Played once, on the first open after it finished.
M5The lantern walk
5.2 s loop while it lasts. Critters at the fire walk the path with star lanterns, staggered 100 ms, a gentle bob each step.
Notes for the developer
States, rules and motion for every screen in this file, then the new push types for 10-03.

20-01
"Share to your chat" on a seeded plan opens the system share sheet with a short link (kithfield.com/p/{slug}, 8 random characters, unguessable). The link is plan-scoped: revoking it (plan menu) or the plan being deleted makes it show "This plan has ended". OG tags: og:image is the 1200×630 card, og:title "{plan} · Kithfield", og:description "{planter} planted this. Pick the days that work, no app needed." Localised from the planter's app language.
Standard share sheet.
card
Rendered server-side on plan change; three states: open (day buds), sprouted (date, time, place), past (pressed flower). Never shows who said no or how many answered. Cache-busted with the plan's version so chat apps re-fetch. Vietnamese uses Lora and Patrick Hand (full tone-mark coverage).
Static image.
20-02
Mobile web, 390 wide, no login. Shows title, place, day buds and avatars of members who said yes, never their names, the chat, photos or the farm. Bud: press and hold 350 ms to pick (tap also works for assistive tech); multi-select. Answer: in / maybe / can't. First name (1–24 chars) and a critter from 6 shown + "More" (the full cast minus critters already taken on this farm). A guest token in a first-party cookie lets them change their answer from the same device. Rate limit 10 answers per link per IP per hour. If guest answers are off (11-05) the page shows the plan and "Ask June to add you".
Bud grows one stage on pick (spring, 300 ms). No page transitions beyond a fade.
20-03
Thanks page with their critter at the gate. One soft line and a quiet "Get Kithfield" (App Store / Play deep link carrying the guest token). No countdown, no "your friends are waiting", no second prompt. On the hangout day the same link shows "Check in" from 2 hours before to 2 hours after.
Critter idles at the gate (art loop).
20-04
Guest answers arrive as vote / rsvp events with actor = guest:{id} and a gate badge in replies. They count toward "enough yeses". A guest check-in counts toward a hangout's Gatherings dew (2+ people). Guests cannot see or send chat. Keepers can remove a guest answer from its row menu.
New reply slides in from the gate side.
20-05
On install with a guest token, onboarding skips the critter pick and the invite steps; the guest becomes a member after a keeper approves (or straight away if the farm allows invites from members). Answers are re-attributed to the new member. A guest who never installs is cleared 30 days after the plan's date.
Reuses the 04-19 move-in walk, starting at the gate.
20-06
Interactive widgets (iOS 17 App Intents; Android Glance). Lit window = opened in the last 3 hours; tapping one sends a firefly (Gestures family, same caps as in-app). Your own window is not a target. Large widget adds the lantern row and "Free tonight", which opens 20-16 as a sheet. Day/night follows local time.
M1. Window glow is a pre-rendered timeline entry, then the widget reloads.
20-07
Small: next plan (bud + date, never "in 3 days"), a friend's lantern (most recent if several, never a count), the fire is lit (names of who's there). Each taps through to its screen.
Static.
20-08
Lock screen: circular critter (your own) glows when something is on your doorstep (gift, slow post, envelope); lantern circular when a friend is free; rectangular says what. StandBy shows the night farm full-bleed with a dim clock; respects StandBy's night red shift.
None; glow is a static state.
20-09
Shown once per person, on the first farm open after their first plan sprouts or their first Sunday harvest, whichever comes first. Not shown if a Kithfield widget is already installed. "Not now" stores widgetAskDismissed; never shown again unprompted. Settings › Widgets keeps the same preview and how-to.
How-to steps advance every 2.2 s; with Reduce Motion they are all shown at once.
20-10
Saturday 18:00 farm time. Push type "Your week" (below). The farm card shows Saturday 18:00 to Sunday 08:00 and disappears after; it never says who has or hasn't added.
Card rises with the sheet.
20-11
Suggestions: photos shared in the chat this week, then (with permission) from the camera roll by date; on-device only. 1–6 per person, optional caption up to 60 characters. Writes scrapbook_page (Gatherings, 1 dew). Edits allowed until Sunday 08:00. No list of contributors, no "3 friends have added".
Check mark pops (spring, 200 ms).
20-12
Composed at 08:00 Sunday with the harvest (07-02): up to 12 photos arranged around the basket by a seeded layout; critters in the corners are those who added, chosen by seed, never all members. If nobody added, there is no page and the harvest shows alone. Long-press a photo: view, save, or (owner) remove.
M3; the page turn is 18-11's two-drawing turn.
20-13
Spines in date order; sprouted hangouts' pressed pages (06) slot between. Empty weeks are not rendered: no placeholder, no gap. Spines show a date, never a count.
A pulled spine tilts out, then the page opens.
20-14
At most one memory a month, only for pages at least 50 weeks old, never on a day with another moment. "Hide this page" by any member hides it from memories and the shelf for everyone; keepers can unhide. Hidden pages are never printed.
Card fades in; no confetti.
20-15
Available from 1 December for the current year and any past year. Leafing through is free for everyone; printing goes to 09-08 (paid print, not Plus).
Pages turn with 18-11.
20-16
Entry points: You tab, large widget, long-press your own critter. Windows end at 18:00, midnight, or Sunday 23:59 farm time. Note up to 40 characters. No location is collected or inferred. One lantern per person at a time; lighting a new one replaces it.
Selected option's lantern lights (M2, first half).
20-17
The lantern hangs by the friend's cottage until its time passes. Push "Free tonight" to the farm (below). No seen-by list; opening the farm does not tell the lantern's owner anything.
Lantern sways (art loop).
20-18
"Me too" lights your lantern for the same window. With 2+ lanterns in the same window, everyone with a lantern sees the card; "Plant it" opens 06-01 prefilled (note as title, today, the end of the window as the latest time). In-app only, no push.
Second lantern lights beside the first.
20-19
When the window ends the lantern dims and is gone by next morning. The owner sees only "has gone out" on their You tab, whatever happened. Never "nobody answered".
M2, second half.
20-20
One project per season, picked by a farm vote in the last week of the previous season (default: next in bridge → bandstand → tree house). Progress = all dew this season ÷ a target scaled to farm size, applied once nightly; stages only go up. The art takes progress 0–1; the UI never shows the number. No per-person contribution is stored for display.
Static by day; new parts appear overnight.
20-21
Reveal on the first open after it finishes. Unlocks: bridge (a new path to the pond, a slow-post bench), bandstand (fire room outdoors on evenings above 18°C), tree house (the owl's new perch, a lookout photo frame). Finished builds stay on the farm for good.

20-22
If a night adds nothing, the builder rests. The build never loses parts, and an unfinished project at the season's end stays in place and carries on next season.
Builder dozes (sleep loop).

20-24

Badges swap at band changes with a fade.
