Description
Stay close to your friends, without the guilt of texting back.
In Kithfield, your group is a little farm and every friend is a critter living on it. A wave, a photo or a quiet “thinking of you” keeps it green.
Nobody keeps score. There are no streaks and no read receipts, and quiet friends’ critters just doze until they’re back.
SAY HI WITHOUT WORDS
Leave a firefly on a friend’s doorstep, a dewdrop on a photo that made you smile, or a note marked “no reply needed”. Every little thing counts.
PLANS THAT ACTUALLY HAPPEN
“We should hang out” becomes a seed. It sprouts once it has a day, a place and enough yeses. On the day, everyone gathers at the campfire.
THE FIRE ROOM
Pull up a log for a voice chat by the fire. You always join muted, and you can just listen.
STICKERS
Hand-drawn stickers of your critters: hugs, waves and tiny celebrations.
OUR RULES
No streaks. No read receipts. No ads, ever. Quiet is always welcome.
OPEN SOURCE
Kithfield’s code is open, so anyone can check how it works. Patrons keep the lights on. Made by oddeggs, the makers of 6pm.

Notes for the developer
Icon Composer
Export each layer as a 1024×1024 SVG (PNG for the painted sky and glow), in this order: 01-sky, 02-hill, 03-cottage, 04-glow, 05-critter. In Icon Composer: sky as the background fill; hill and cottage as one group with a light specular; glow set to "no translucency" so it stays the brightest value in clear and tinted; critter on top. Provide dark-mode overrides for sky (#3A2A3C → #A0605A) and hill (#3C4A32).
Alternate icons
iOS needs every alternate icon bundled in the app, so "your critter" ships as 16 critters × 3 looks (default coat with a scarf, beanie or bow), named AltIcon-{critter}-{look}, plus AltIcon-Season-Spring/Summer/Autumn/Winter: 52 icons, about 1.6 MB. The closest look to your critter is pre-selected; exact coats and outfits appear on the widget instead. Set with setAlternateIconName (Expo: expo-dynamic-app-icon). Patron perk; lapsed patrons keep the icon they chose.
Android
Adaptive icon: ic_launcher_background = sky + hill (108dp), ic_launcher_foreground = cottage + glow + critter inside the 66dp safe zone. Monochrome: ic_launcher_monochrome, cottage with the window cut out and the critter silhouette inside. Android 12 splash: windowSplashScreenAnimatedIcon is an AnimatedVectorDrawable of the window lighting up over 700ms; background #F5EEE2 / night #241E26.
expo-splash-screen
["expo-splash-screen", {
  "backgroundColor": "#F5EEE2",
  "image": "./assets/splash/horizon-day.png",
  "resizeMode": "cover",
  "dark": { "backgroundColor": "#241E26", "image": "./assets/splash/horizon-night.png" },
  "android": { "image": "./assets/splash/icon-window.png", "imageWidth": 200,
               "backgroundColor": "#F5EEE2", "dark": { "backgroundColor": "#241E26" } }
}]
Call SplashScreen.preventAutoHideAsync() on launch, then hideAsync() once the reveal canvas has drawn its first frame (same paper seed 9901), so the hand-off is invisible. Warm start and Reduce Motion skip the reveal: 120ms and 200ms cross-fades.
Colours
Launch background day #F5EEE2, night #241E26. Icon dusk sky #E9A88A → #F2C49A → #F8E2C0; dark sky #3A2A3C → #6A4352 → #A0605A; window light #FFE08A (always the lightest value).
Store art
The panorama is one 7740 × 2796 painting (six 1290-wide frames). Export it from kithfield-art.js scenes pano + panofront at 3×, with the phone UI composited between them, then slice at 1290px. Night set: night="true". Single frames: name="0" to "5".
Store rules
Screenshots show real UI only (chat, tend tray, widget). Headlines in Young Serif, kickers in Gaegu; Vietnamese uses Lora and Patrick Hand. Keep text out of the bottom 15% on Play.
